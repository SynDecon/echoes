01

The Light

光
中文

The Sun is 150 million kilometers away. Light travels 300,000 kilometers every second — the fastest thing there is. And yet it still takes eight minutes and twenty seconds to reach us.

So when we look at the Sun, we are not seeing it now. We are seeing it as it was eight minutes ago. The stars are farther still: some of their light has been traveling for hundreds, even thousands of years. When we look up at the night sky, we are looking into the past.

What we see is almost never what is happening now. Between the moment something happens and the moment we see it, there is a gap.

And that gap does not exist only in the universe.

太阳距离我们约1.5亿公里。光每秒走30万公里，这是宇宙中最快的速度。可即便如此，它仍需要8分20秒，才能抵达地球。

所以，当我们看见太阳，看见的并不是此刻的太阳，而是八分钟以前的它。星星更远：有些星光已经走了数百年，甚至数千年。我们仰望夜空，看到的其实是过去。

原来，我们所看见的，几乎从不是此刻正在发生的事。在事情发生与被看见之间，存在着一段距离。

而这种距离，并不只在宇宙之中。

02I

The Perspective

视角
中文

And just like light, our understanding is limited by another kind of distance — the place where we stand.

In the middle of an argument, we feel our own hurt first, and struggle to see the other person’s. Facing a decision whose outcome is still unknown, we can only judge with what we know at the time. We cannot tell which small detail will turn out to matter.

Sometimes, clarity comes too late.

就像光一样，我们的认识也受到另一种距离的限制——我们所站的位置。

身处一场争执，我们往往先看见自己的委屈，却难以同时理解对方的处境；面对尚未揭晓的选择，我们只能依据当时掌握的信息判断，却不知道哪些细节，会在未来变得重要。

有时，清晰来得太晚。

02II

The Perspective

视角
中文

Su Shi, a Chinese poet, once wrote:

“I cannot see the true face of Mount Lu, because I am standing within it.”

Sometimes we cannot see the whole picture — not because it is hidden, but because we are standing inside it.

Changing our position can mean leaving a familiar place. More often, it simply takes time to arrive somewhere we could not stand before.

苏轼曾写道：

「不识庐山真面目，只缘身在此山中。」

有时，看不清并不是因为事情藏得太深，而是因为我们正身处其中。改变位置，有时意味着走出熟悉的环境；更多时候，它只是需要时间，让人慢慢走到从前站不到的地方。

03I

The Years

岁月
中文

Some things only show their weight after time has passed.

When someone we love leaves, it often happens in a few ordinary words. It took me years to understand that they were really gone.

Only when those familiar voices stopped did I understand that the ordinary days I had taken for granted were already gone.

有些事情，只有经过时间，才会显现出它原本的分量。

亲人朋友的离开，往往只有一句轻飘飘的话。可我却花了好几年，才真正明白，他们已经不在了。

直到熟悉的声音不再响起，才发现，那些以为寻常的日子，早已走远。

03II

The Years

岁月
中文

Nalan Xingde, a Chinese poet, once wrote:

“I thought it was just an ordinary day — at the time.”

What time teaches us is not only about loss. It also lets us see, again, what we already had.

I used to believe that if two people were close enough, there should be no distance between them. I have come to think the opposite: a relationship that lasts does not hold the other person in place. It lets them stay free.

纳兰性德曾写道：

「当时只道是寻常。」

时间带来的理解不只关于失去，也让我们重新看见曾经拥有的东西。

我曾以为，足够亲近，就不该有距离；后来才明白，真正稳定的关系，不是把对方留在原地，而是允许对方保有自己的自由。

04I

The Self

自我
中文
“Life can only be understood backwards, but it must be lived forwards.”

— Søren Kierkegaard

With time, the pieces of the past we could not understand begin to fit together — seen from a new angle.

But time does not guarantee that we understand correctly. We have gained the experience we lacked, and we have also gained the limits of who we are now. We can look back and examine what happened. We may never see it exactly as it was.

「生命只能向后理解，却必须向前生活。」

——克尔凯郭尔

时间久了，过去那些我们无法理解的片段，开始从新的角度拼合起来。

但时间并不保证理解一定正确。如今的自己多了几分经验，也多了此刻的局限：我们能够回头审视，却未必能够还原当时的原貌。

04II

The Self

自我
中文

The past has not changed. What has changed is the person looking back at it.

To understand the past is also to understand ourselves.

Every time we look back, we see not only what happened, but also the person we have become.

If we can now understand the road behind us, how will the person we become look back at who we are today?

过去没有改变，改变的是那个回头看过去的人。

理解过去，也是在认识自己。

每一次回望，我们看到的不只是发生过什么，还有自己成为了怎样的人。

如果今天的我们，已经能够理解自己走过的路，那么未来的自己，又会如何看待今天？

05

Echoes

回声
中文

The starry sky we see is itself an echo from the past.

That light set out from somewhere distant, traveled through time, and arrived here — in our present.

An echo is a sound that reaches us after it has already ended.

We do not know how our future selves will understand today, or what the things we live through now will finally mean. But perhaps we do not need to know yet.

The light in the universe comes from distant stars. The light that lets us understand ourselves comes from a much more distant future. Some moments have already ended, and yet they have not truly reached us.

They are still on their way.

我们看见的星空，本身就是一片来自过去的回声。

那些光从遥远的地方出发，穿越时间，抵达此刻的我们。

回声，是声音结束后才抵达我们。

我们不知道未来的自己会怎样理解今天，也不知道此刻的经历最终意味着什么。但或许，不必急着知道。

宇宙的光来自遥远的星辰；而看清自己的光，来自更远的未来。有些瞬间已经结束，却尚未真正抵达我们。

它们仍在路上。
